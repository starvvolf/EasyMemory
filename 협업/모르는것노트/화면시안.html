Viewing-Coordinate Parameters

Three-Dimensional Viewing · p.3

To obtain a view of a scene, we first select a viewing position in world coordinates, called the view reference point P₀. We then choose the direction of the view-plane normal vector N, and a view-up vector V that establishes the positive direction for the y-axis of the viewing frame.

Only objects inside the view volume are displayed. For a perspective projection, the view volume is a frustum: a pyramid whose top has been cut off by the near clipping plane. Positions outside this region are removed by the clipping routines before the normalization transformation is applied.

The complete 3D viewing pipeline therefore proceeds from modeling coordinates to world coordinates, then to viewing coordinates, then through the projection transformation to normalized coordinates, and finally to device coordinates.
